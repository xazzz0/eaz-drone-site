What to include.

Send the property or site address, requested service, access arrangements, and target timing. Include any photo, video, or delivery requirements.

For construction-progress coverage, include:

  • Site address or pin, approximate size, and current project stage.
  • Requested aerial photos or video, and any views you want covered.
  • Preferred visit frequency, start date, and expected project duration.
  • Site contact, access arrangements, and delivery requirements.

For real estate or vacation-rental media, include:

  • Property address, approximate size, and listing or rental use.
  • Which media you need: aerial photos, aerial video, interiors, or exteriors.
  • Access contact, availability, and any guest turnover window.
  • Listing date or delivery deadline, plus any required formats.
